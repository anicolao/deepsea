A little courage. A little restraint.

Treasure is only yours
if you make it back.

Play with 2–6 friends, each on their own device. Head deeper for treasure, or turn back toward the submarine.

Everyone shares one oxygen supply. The treasure you carry slows you down and uses more air. Bring back the most value over three dives to win.
