## Scrolling descent

The built-in image generation tool created `ocean-depths.png` as the style reference and the three final `ocean-shallows.png`, `ocean-twilight.png` and `ocean-abyss.png` sections on 2026-09-12. Each is 724 × 2172 pixels. The browser uses quality-90 `.webp` delivery copies of the three sections, preloaded on arrival. Reproduce each with `nix develop -c cwebp -q 90 -m 6 static/art/ocean-<zone>.png -o static/art/ocean-<zone>.webp`.

The sections meet at matching water colors and fade into one tall scene inside the full treasure-path content. They scroll with the chits and never repeat. Each section uses proportional cover sizing, with the surface anchored at the top and the abyss framed to retain its anglerfish and bioluminescent life on wide screens; animals retain their proportions on phone, desktop and shorter later-dive paths. The descent moves from sunlit schooling fish and a ray to twilight jellyfish and squid, then an anglerfish and bioluminescent life in the midnight abyss. Submarine and diver labels use solid navy backplates for readability in the brighter shallows.

### Style reference prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production background artwork for a vertically scrolling underwater board-game path. Create one exceptionally tall portrait illustration, 1024 x 3072 or 1:3 aspect ratio. A continuous descent from the sunlit ocean surface at the very top into the midnight abyss at the bottom. Match a sophisticated illustrated board game: paper grain, soft painterly cut-paper shapes, restrained teal and navy palette. Top quarter: rippling turquoise water, shafts of light and small schooling silver fish. Second quarter: dimmer blue open water with a distant ray and sparse fish. Third quarter: very dark indigo twilight water, delicate translucent jellyfish and a small squid with subtle luminous points. Bottom quarter: near-black blue abyss with a small anglerfish, sparse tiny bioluminescent organisms and dark rocky seafloor only at the very bottom. Brightness must progressively decrease with depth, no return to bright water in lower areas. Keep animals elegant, small and mainly near left and right edges. The center 65 percent must remain calm open water for overlaid treasure tokens and a winding path, with visible subtle atmospheric texture. No kelp or sunlit coral in the abyss. No text, borders, UI, tokens, divers, submarine, diagram labels or watermarks. This is the artwork only, not a screenshot or mockup.
